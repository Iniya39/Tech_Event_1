01 — THE INCIDENT

At approximately 21:40, a restricted computer system inside the control room transmitted a short encrypted message to an unknown destination.

INVESTIGATION NOTE:

In this investigation, the term SIGNAL refers to an encrypted digital transmission sent by the control system.

The transmission was not a voice recording, radio message, or physical alarm.

It was a digital message sent through the system.

At the time of the transmission, the control room was believed to be empty.

However, system records show that the terminal became active shortly before the transmission.

👤

02 — UNKNOWN USER

The identity of the person responsible for the system activity is unknown.

At 21:39, an unknown user successfully accessed the system through an external network gateway.

Because the access occurred through the network, there is no evidence that the person physically entered the control room.

At 21:40, the encrypted transmission was sent.

Shortly afterward, the remote connection ended.

CORE INVESTIGATION SCOPE:

The investigation must determine what actually happened from the evidence. Do not make unverified assumptions about the identity of the user.
